# JGS Design System — "Obsidian Street Luxury & Precision Telemetry"

## 1. Visual Identity Principles
- **Canvas**: Deep Midnight Plum (`#09060F`) and Obsidian Velvet (`#110B1C`) surfaces prevent eye strain during extended chart and calculator use.
- **Accents**: Metallic Crown Gold (`#D4AF37`) for primary actions, key thresholds, and active indicators; Royal Purple (`#7E22CE`) and Mascot Lavender (`#A882DD`) for structural depth.
- **Typography**:
  - `Space Grotesk` for geometric, authoritative display headers.
  - `Plus Jakarta Sans` for beginner-friendly long-form reading.
  - `JetBrains Mono` (`tabular-nums`) for all prices, lot sizes, percentages, and $R$-multiples.

## 2. Anti-Clutter & Anti-Hype Guardrails
- No neon cyberpunk glow overload.
- No fake live profit popups or fabricated social proof notifications.
- Selective, purposeful usage of Jey (`public/images/jey-mascot.png`) as a senior risk strategist mentor.
